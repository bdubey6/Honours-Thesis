# Honours Thesis Proposal

**Title:** Muscle Coactivation Levels Reflect a Tradeoff Between Metabolic Cost and Performance When
Responding to Physical Disturbances During Upper Limb Posture Control
**Author:** Bhuvan Dubey
**Date:** January 2025

[Read the proposal (PDF)](./Honours-Proposal-FINAL.pdf) · [Word source](./Honours-Proposal-FINAL.docx)

## Summary

This proposal lays out the honours thesis project studying the trade-off between muscle coactivation,
metabolic cost, and task performance during upper-limb posture control. It extends the summer research
literature review with a full research question, hypothesis, and rationale section, and previews the
planned Kinarm exoskeleton / EMG / gas-respirometry methodology (the same design later reported in the
[Summer Research poster](../../Summer-Research/Poster) and its [KNES 590 expansion](../Poster)).

**Research question:** How does muscle coactivation affect the trade-off between performance and
metabolic cost during upper limb posture control with mechanical disturbances?

**Hypothesis:** There is a trade-off between muscle coactivation, metabolic cost, and task performance.
