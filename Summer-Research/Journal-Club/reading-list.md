# Journal Club Reading List (2024)

Cluff Lab, Faculty of Kinesiology, University of Calgary. Grouped by theme; items with notes in
[`notes/`](./notes) are marked ✅.

## Muscle coactivation and muscle stiffness
- Burdet, E., Osu, R., Franklin, D. W., Milner, T. E., & Kawato, M. (2001). The central nervous system
  stabilizes unstable dynamics by learning optimal impedance. *Nature*, 414(6862), 446–449. ✅ [notes](./notes/burdet-2001-franklin-2004-impedance-control.md)
- Hogan, N. (1984). Adaptive control of mechanical impedance by coactivation of antagonist muscles. *IEEE
  Transactions on Automatic Control*, 29(8), 681–690.
- Franklin, D. W., So, U., Kawato, M., & Milner, T. E. (2004). Impedance control balances stability with
  metabolically costly muscle activation. *Journal of Neurophysiology*, 92(5), 3097–3105. ✅ [notes](./notes/burdet-2001-franklin-2004-impedance-control.md)
- Franklin, D. W., Liaw, G., Milner, T. E., Osu, R., Burdet, E., & Kawato, M. (2007). Endpoint stiffness of
  the arm is directionally tuned to instability in the environment. *Journal of Neuroscience*, 27(29),
  7705–7716.
- Franklin, D. W., & Wolpert, D. M. (2011). Computational mechanisms of sensorimotor control. *Neuron*,
  72(3), 425–442.
- Horslen, B. C., Milburn, G. N., Blum, K. P., Simha, S. N., Campbell, K. S., & Ting, L. H. (2023).
  History-dependent muscle resistance to stretch remains high after small, posturally relevant
  pre-movements. *Journal of Experimental Biology*, 226(18). ✅ [notes](./notes/horslen-2023-muscle-thixotropy.md)

## Muscle coactivation and responses to sensory feedback
- Crevecoeur, F., Scott, S. H., & Cluff, T. (2019). Robust control in human reaching movements: a
  model-free strategy to compensate for unpredictable disturbances. *Journal of Neuroscience*, 39(41),
  8135–8148.
- Maurus, P., Jackson, K., Cashaback, J. G., & Cluff, T. (2023). The nervous system tunes sensorimotor
  gains when reaching in variable mechanical environments. *iScience*, 26(6). ✅ [notes](./notes/maurus-2023-sensorimotor-gains.md)
- Kalidindi, H. T., & Crevecoeur, F. (2023). Human reaching control in dynamic environments. *Current
  Opinion in Neurobiology*, 83, 102810.
- Crevecoeur, F., & Scott, S. H. (2014). Beyond muscle stiffness: importance of state-estimation to account
  for very fast motor corrections. *PLoS Computational Biology*, 10(10), e1003869.
- Saliba, C. M., Rainbow, M. J., Selbie, W. S., Deluzio, K. J., & Scott, S. H. (2020). Co-contraction uses
  dual control of agonist-antagonist muscles to improve motor performance. *bioRxiv*, 2020-03.
- Martino, G., Beck, O. N., & Ting, L. H. (2023). Voluntary muscle coactivation in quiet standing elicits
  reciprocal rather than coactive agonist-antagonist control of reactive balance. *Journal of
  Neurophysiology*, 129(6), 1378–1388.
