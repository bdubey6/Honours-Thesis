# Journal Club

Notes from journal club sessions during summer research in the Cluff Lab (Faculty of Kinesiology,
University of Calgary), covering the motor control / sensorimotor feedback literature underlying the
[muscle coactivation poster project](../Poster).

- [`reading-list.md`](./reading-list.md) — full reading list, grouped by theme.
- [`notes/`](./notes) — per-paper discussion notes (research question, methods, results, discussion, and
  open questions raised during discussion).
- [`question-session-muscle-spindle-anatomy.md`](./notes/question-session-muscle-spindle-anatomy.md) —
  foundational neurophysiology notes (muscle spindle anatomy, stretch reflex arc, proprioceptive pathways)
  from a background Q&A session.

Papers themselves are not included here (most are not open access) — see the reading list for full
citations and DOIs where available.
