# Literature Review

Summer research literature review on muscle coactivation, written to accompany the
[Cluff Lab coactivation project](../Poster) (University of Calgary, August 2024).

## Final version

**[`Literature-Review-FINAL.pdf`](./Literature-Review-FINAL.pdf)** (17 pages) — adds a new section,
*"Interplay Between Muscle Coactivation and Sensory Feedback"* (covering corticomotor excitability
and clinical populations, e.g. multiple sclerosis), and cleans up a couple of reference citations
relative to the draft.

## Drafts (`Drafts/`)

1. **`01-Literature-Review-Draft.pdf`** (15 pages) — original draft.

## Structure

- Introduction
- Physiological Mechanisms of Muscle Coactivation
- Modulating Joint Stiffness Through Coactivation
- Variability in Coactivation Across Individuals and Contexts
- Balancing Performance and Metabolic Cost
- Interplay Between Muscle Coactivation and Sensory Feedback *(updated version only)*
- Implications for Rehabilitation and Clinical Applications
- Future Research Directions
- Conclusion
- Methodology (Kinarm setup, participant preparation, task protocols — matches the
  [poster](../Poster) and [data](../Data))
- References
