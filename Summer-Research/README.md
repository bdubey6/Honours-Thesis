# Summer Research — Cluff Lab

Summer research completed in Tyler Cluff's lab (Faculty of Kinesiology / Hotchkiss Brain Institute,
University of Calgary), investigating the metabolic cost / performance tradeoff of muscle coactivation
during upper-limb posture control.

## Contents

- [`Literature-Review/`](./Literature-Review) — literature review on muscle coactivation.
- [`Poster/`](./Poster) — original conference poster (n = 5): *Muscle Coactivation Levels Reflect a
  Tradeoff Between Metabolic Cost and Performance When Responding to Physical Disturbances During Upper
  Limb Posture Control*.
- [`Data/`](./Data) — de-identified per-participant anthropometric and EMG data.
